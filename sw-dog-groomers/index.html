Our Story

Your Pet's Second Home

At SW Dog Groomers, we believe every dog and cat deserves to feel pampered and cared for. Our team of certified groomers combines professional expertise with genuine love for animals. We use only premium, hypoallergenic products and take time to understand each pet's unique personality and needs. Our Puppy University Programme is designed to help young puppies develop positive associations with grooming from the start, while our pet taxi service removes the stress of transport. Since opening in Leopold Road, we've become the trusted choice for pet parents across South West London.
