About Us

La Taberna Leeds

Located in the heart of Leeds city centre at Britannia House, La Taberna Leeds brings the warmth, flavours, and hospitality of Spain to Yorkshire. Our restaurant has been a beloved destination for authentic Spanish cuisine for over 15 years.

We pride ourselves on sourcing only the finest ingredients, many imported directly from Spain, to ensure every dish transports you to the Spanish countryside. Our experienced team combines traditional cooking techniques with contemporary presentation.

  • Authentic Spanish recipes passed down through generations
  • Premium ingredients imported directly from Spain
  • Expert sommelier-curated wine collection
  • Warm, welcoming atmosphere perfect for all occasions
  • Over 15 years of serving Leeds residents and visitors
  • Dedicated team of trained chefs and hospitality professionals
