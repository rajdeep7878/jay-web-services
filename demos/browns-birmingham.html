About Browns Birmingham

Nestled in the historic St Martins Square in Birmingham's vibrant city centre, Browns Birmingham stands as a beacon of culinary excellence. Our restaurant combines traditional fine dining techniques with modern innovation to create unforgettable meals.

With over 15 years of dedicated service, we've earned our reputation as one of Birmingham's premier dining destinations. Our team of passionate chefs, knowledgeable sommeliers, and attentive staff work tirelessly to ensure every visit exceeds expectations.
