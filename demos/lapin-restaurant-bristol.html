About Lapin Restaurant Bristol

Lapin Restaurant Bristol represents 15 years of culinary passion and dedication to excellence. Located in the vibrant Cargo 2 complex on Museum Street, we've become Bristol's premier destination for authentic French fine dining.

Our name, "Lapin," means rabbit in French—a nod to the classic French dish lapin à la moutarde. This reflects our commitment to traditional French cuisine reimagined for contemporary tastes.

"We believe that great food is about more than taste—it's about creating memories. Every dish, every wine selection, every interaction with our guests is an opportunity to exceed expectations."

Our team of trained French chefs brings decades of combined experience to our kitchen. We source the finest ingredients from trusted suppliers, both locally and internationally, to ensure every plate meets our exacting standards.
