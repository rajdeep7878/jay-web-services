Our Story

Excellence in Every Plate

OMA stands as a beacon of culinary excellence in London's vibrant food scene. Our name—a nod to thoughtful simplicity—reflects our philosophy: let quality ingredients and masterful technique speak for themselves.

Located on the historic Bedale Street in Southwark, our restaurant combines contemporary design with warm hospitality. We're committed to sourcing the finest ingredients from trusted suppliers and working closely with local producers whenever possible.

Why Choose OMA

  • Michelin-trained chefs with international experience
