About Us

Macintyre's Hair & Beauty

Established Excellence in Edinburgh

For over 15 years, Macintyre's Hair & Beauty has been Edinburgh's trusted destination for professional hair and beauty services. Located on prestigious Thistle Street, we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques.

Our team of experienced stylists and beauty therapists are passionate about delivering transformative results. We use only premium products and stay current with the latest trends and techniques.

  • Award-winning stylists with 15+ years experience
  • Premium professional hair and beauty products
  • Personalised consultations for every client
  • Relaxing, welcoming salon environment
  • Competitive pricing with loyalty rewards
  • Located in heart of Edinburgh city centre
