About Hideaway London

A Sanctuary in the Heart of Mayfair

Hideaway London was created with one simple vision: to offer a peaceful retreat from the energy of the city. Nestled on Mount Street in Mayfair, our intimate 45-seat café combines exceptional specialty coffee with carefully curated light meals in a warm, inviting space.

Founded in 2024, we've quickly become a favourite among locals, remote workers, and coffee enthusiasts who appreciate quality, authenticity, and genuine hospitality. Every detail—from our single-origin beans to our design—has been thoughtfully selected.
