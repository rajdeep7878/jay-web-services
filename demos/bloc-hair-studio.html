About BLOC Hair Studio

Your Local Hair Experts

Based in the heart of Sheffield at Unit 2, Beton House on Rhodes Street, BLOC Hair Studio has been delivering exceptional hair care to the local community for over a decade. Our team of experienced stylists are passionate about creating cuts, colours, and treatments that make you feel confident and beautiful.

We believe in combining professional expertise with genuine care for every client. Using only premium products and staying up-to-date with the latest techniques, we ensure you receive salon-standard results every single time.

  • Experienced team of certified stylists
  • Premium, professional-grade products
  • Latest cutting and colouring techniques
  • Welcoming atmosphere for all ages
  • Competitive pricing and loyalty rewards
  • Easy parking and convenient location
