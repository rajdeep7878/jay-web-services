Our Heritage

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

For over 25 years, Clos Maggiore has been London's most cherished destination for fine French dining. Located in the vibrant heart of Covent Garden at 33 King Street, our intimate restaurant combines classical French techniques with contemporary creativity.

Our Executive Chef and team are passionate about sourcing the finest ingredients and crafting each dish with meticulous attention to detail. With Michelin recognition and countless awards, we take pride in delivering an unforgettable culinary experience that honours both tradition and innovation.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or enjoying an intimate dinner, Clos Maggiore offers an enchanting escape into the world of fine dining.
