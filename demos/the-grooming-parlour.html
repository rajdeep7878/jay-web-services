What We Offer

Professional Grooming Services

We provide comprehensive dog grooming services tailored to your pet's breed, coat type, and individual needs. From routine maintenance to full transformations.

✨

Full Grooming

Complete wash, dry, brush, and cut service. Includes nail trim, ear cleaning, and anal gland expression. Ideal for maintaining your dog's health and appearance.

✂️

Breed-Specific Cuts

Expert trimming tailored to your dog's breed standards. Whether it's a poodle clip, terrier style, or custom design, we've got the expertise.

🛁

Bath & Wash

Luxurious bath with premium shampoo and conditioner. Perfect for dogs who just need a refresh or prior to other grooming services.

💅

Nail Care

Professional nail trimming and filing. We take extra care with anxious dogs and ensure proper nail health and comfort.

👂

Ear & Eye Care

Gentle ear cleaning and plucking for breeds prone to ear issues. Eye area cleaning for tear stain reduction and hygiene.

🎨

Creative Grooming

Special occasion styling, colour-safe dyes, and creative cuts. Perfect for dog shows, photoshoots, or simply for fun!

About Us

Dedicated to Your Dog's Care

The Grooming Parlour has been serving the Putney and South West London community for over a decade. We're passionate about providing exceptional grooming services that keep your furry friends looking and feeling their best.

Our experienced groomers are trained in all breeds and coat types. We take pride in our calm, gentle approach, especially with nervous or anxious dogs. Every pet deserves to leave looking beautiful and feeling happy.
