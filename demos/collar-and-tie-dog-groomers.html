From basic baths to complete grooming packages, we offer comprehensive services tailored to your dog's breed, size, and individual needs.

About Us

Your Dog's Trusted Grooming Partner

Collar And Tie Dog Groomers was founded on a simple principle: every dog deserves professional, compassionate grooming care. With over 15 years of experience, we've become Hall Green's trusted choice for dog grooming.

Our team of certified, experienced groomers treats every dog with the utmost care and patience. We understand that each dog is unique, with different temperaments, coat types, and grooming needs.

"We don't just groom dogs – we care for them. Every appointment is an opportunity to make your furry friend feel loved and pampered."

We use only premium, pet-safe grooming products and maintain the highest standards of hygiene and safety. Whether your dog needs a basic bath or a complete transformation, Collar And Tie is here to deliver excellence.
