Who We Are

Urban Edinburgh Salon

Since opening our doors on Howe Street, Urban Edinburgh has become Edinburgh's trusted destination for exceptional hair care. We're passionate about helping our clients look and feel their best through quality service and genuine care.

Our team of experienced stylists combines technical expertise with creative vision, ensuring every client leaves feeling confident and beautiful. We believe in building long-term relationships with our clients and understanding their unique hair needs.
