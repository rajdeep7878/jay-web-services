Professional Hair Services

We offer a comprehensive range of hair services designed to enhance your natural beauty and keep your hair looking its best.

Haircuts & Trims

Precision cuts tailored to your face shape and hair type. From classic styles to modern trends, our stylists create the perfect cut for you.

🎨

Colour & Highlights

Professional hair colouring, root touch-ups, balayage, and custom colour treatments using premium products that nourish while they colour.

💆

About Renegade Hair Studio

Since 2009, Renegade Hair Studio has been Leeds' trusted destination for exceptional hair care. We believe that great hair starts with listening to our clients and understanding their unique needs, lifestyle and aspirations.

Our team of passionate, professionally trained stylists stay up-to-date with the latest techniques, trends and products. We invest in ongoing training to deliver cutting-edge services while maintaining the warm, welcoming atmosphere our clients love.

  • Award-winning stylists with 15+ years collective experience
  • Premium professional hair products and treatments
  • Comfortable, modern salon in Leeds city centre
  • Flexible booking and same-day appointments available
  • Personalised consultation for every client
  • Competitive pricing with loyalty rewards
