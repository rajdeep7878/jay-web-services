Our Story

Welcome to La Piola Italian

La Piola Italian brings the warmth, flavour, and hospitality of Italy to the heart of Leeds. Our restaurant is built on a passion for authentic cuisine and genuine Italian tradition.

Every dish is created with care, using recipes passed down through generations and ingredients sourced directly from Italy. We believe in honest cooking, exceptional service, and creating memorable dining experiences.

Whether you're joining us for a casual lunch, celebrating a special occasion, or enjoying an intimate dinner, we welcome you to our table as family.

  • Authentic Italian recipes & techniques
  • Premium sourced ingredients
  • Warm, family-oriented hospitality
  • Private dining & event spaces available
  • Expert wine pairings & recommendations
