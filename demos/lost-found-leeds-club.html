About Us

Crafting Memories Since 2009

Lost & Found Leeds Club has established itself as the premier dining destination in Leeds city centre. Our commitment to excellence, innovation, and hospitality has earned us recognition as one of Yorkshire's finest restaurants.

Our team of passionate chefs, sommeliers, and hospitality professionals work tirelessly to create unforgettable dining experiences for every guest who walks through our doors.

  • Award-winning cuisine and chef-led kitchen
  • Extensive wine collection with expert sommelier
  • Elegant private dining facilities available
  • Bespoke event planning and catering services
  • Commitment to locally-sourced ingredients
  • Professional and attentive service team
