Our Story

Bringing Italy to Manchester

For over two decades, Vincenzo Trattoria has been a beacon of authentic Italian cuisine in Manchester. Our restaurant is more than just a place to eat — it's a celebration of Italian culture, tradition, and the art of fine dining.

Every dish on our menu tells a story, crafted by Chef Vincenzo using recipes passed down through generations, combined with the freshest seasonal ingredients sourced from trusted suppliers. From our hand-made pasta to our signature risottos, each element is prepared with meticulous attention to detail.

We believe in creating memorable experiences. Our warm ambiance, attentive service, and passion for Italian hospitality ensure that every visit becomes a treasured memory.

Menu Highlights

From classic antipasti to sumptuous mains, each dish celebrates the essence of Italian gastronomy

🍝

Pasta Fresca

Hand-rolled and hand-cut pasta made daily using traditional methods. Choose from our signature sauces including our famous Ragù Bolognese and Cacio e Pepe.

🍤

Seafood Selection

Fresh seafood delivered daily from quality suppliers. Prepare your palate for our Branzino al Forno, Scallops, and delicate Prawn Risotto creations.

🥩

Signature Mains

Premium cuts prepared with Italian precision. Experience our Osso Buco, Saltimbocca, and slow-cooked specialities that celebrate the finest ingredients.

🍷

Wine Collection

Carefully selected Italian wines from renowned regions including Tuscany, Piedmont, and Sicily. Our sommeliers will guide perfect pairings.

🧀

Cheese & Charcuterie

Artisanal Italian cheeses and cured meats. Start your meal with our carefully curated selection of Parmigiano-Reggiano, Prosciutto, and Salami.

🍰

Dolci & Desserts

Traditional Italian desserts including Tiramisu, Panna Cotta, and our homemade Gelato. Sweet conclusions to exceptional meals.
