Our Offerings

Professional Grooming Services

We offer a full range of dog grooming services tailored to your pet's specific needs. Every pup receives individual attention and care.

🛁

Full Grooming

Complete bath, haircut, nail trim, ear cleaning, and styling. Includes premium shampoo and conditioning treatments suitable for all coat types.

✂️

Bath & Dry

Professional bathing with premium shampoos, conditioning, and thorough blow-dry. Includes nail trim and paw pad cleaning.

💅

Nail Care

Professional nail trimming, filing, and paw pad maintenance. Keeps your dog comfortable and prevents mobility issues.

👂

Ear & Eye Care

Thorough ear cleaning and eye area grooming. Helps prevent infections and keeps your pup looking fresh and clean.

🐾

Specialty Treatments

De-shedding treatments, hydrating masks, flea & tick baths, and therapeutic grooming for senior dogs and special needs.

💆

De-matting

Expert de-matting and detangling for long-haired breeds. We work gently to preserve your dog's coat health.
