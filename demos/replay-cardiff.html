What We Offer

Our Hair Services

From classic cuts to bold colour transformations, Replay Cardiff offers a full range of professional hair services tailored to your style and needs.

✂️

Precision Cuts

Expert cutting techniques for men and women. Whether you're after a sharp fade, textured crop, or elegant long-hair cut, our stylists will create the perfect shape for your hair type and face.

🎨

Colour Services

From subtle highlights to bold transformations. We use premium colour products to deliver vibrant, long-lasting results. Balayage, ombré, full colour, or root touch-ups — we've got you covered.

💆

Styling & Treatments

Professional blow-dries, special occasion styling, and restorative hair treatments. Our deep conditioning and repair packages restore shine and strength to damaged or tired hair.

👶

Children's Hair Care

Friendly, patient service for our younger clients. We make the experience fun and stress-free, perfect for first haircuts and regular maintenance.

🔄

Hair Extensions

Add length and volume with our professional extension services. We offer multiple methods with expert application and personalised aftercare guidance.

✨

Perms & Waves

Create beautiful texture and hold with modern perming and wave treatments. We use advanced techniques for natural-looking, manageable results.
