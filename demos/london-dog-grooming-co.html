Our Services

Expert Grooming for Every Dog

We offer comprehensive grooming services tailored to your dog's breed, size, and specific needs. Every dog receives individual attention and care.

✂️

Full Grooming

Complete bath, haircut, nail trim, ear cleaning, and styling. Perfect for regular maintenance and breed-standard cuts.

🛁

Bath & Wash

Gentle bathing with premium shampoos suitable for all skin types. Includes drying and basic trimming.

💅

Nail Care

Professional nail trimming and grinding. Includes pad cleaning and paw care to keep your dog comfortable.

👂

Ear & Eye Care

Ear cleaning, eye wiping, and facial grooming. Special attention to sensitive areas and breed-specific needs.

💇

Breed-Specific Cuts

Expert styling for all breeds including Doodles, Poodles, Terriers, and more. Show-standard quality available.

🧴

De-shedding Treatment

Intensive treatment to reduce shedding. Includes undercoat removal and conditioning for healthier coat.
