Our Story

We're Passionate About Dogs

Pawfect Style Dog Grooming Spa was built on one simple idea: every dog deserves to be treated like the VIP they truly are. Our experienced groomers — led by Sophie and her dedicated team — combine professional technique with genuine love for animals. We use only natural, skin-friendly products suited to every coat type, and we take the time to understand each dog's individual needs and temperament. Whether your pup is a first-timer or a regular, they're always in safe, caring hands at our Sipson salon.
