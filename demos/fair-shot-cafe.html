About Us

Fair Shot Cafe Story

Fair Shot Cafe was founded on the belief that exceptional coffee should be accessible to everyone. We partner directly with ethical coffee producers across Africa, South America, and Asia to bring you the finest specialty-grade beans.

Located in the vibrant heart of Covent Garden, our cafe is more than just a coffee shop—it's a gathering place where baristas share their passion for craft, where locals catch up with friends, and where every visit feels special.

We're committed to sustainability, quality, and creating a welcoming community space where everyone feels at home.

  • Ethically sourced, single-origin beans
  • Expert barista training and certification
  • Sustainable and eco-friendly practices
  • Community events and coffee tastings
