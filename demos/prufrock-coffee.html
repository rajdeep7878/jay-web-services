Who We Are

About Prufrock Coffee

Established over 15 years ago, Prufrock Coffee has become a beloved destination for coffee enthusiasts and professionals across London. Located in the heart of Leather Lane, we've built our reputation on quality, consistency, and genuine hospitality.

Our passion for coffee begins with sourcing the finest beans from ethical, sustainable growers worldwide. Each cup is prepared with meticulous attention to detail, whether it's a traditional espresso or a perfectly steamed cappuccino.

We believe a great café is more than just coffee—it's a space where people connect, create, and belong. Our team is dedicated to making every visit memorable.

  • Specialty-grade, ethically sourced coffee beans
  • Professional baristas with years of experience
  • Free Wi-Fi and comfortable workspace
  • Events and coffee tasting sessions
  • Sustainable, eco-friendly practices
  • Welcoming community space
