About Us

Indigo & Co Hair Collective

Indigo & Co Hair Collective is a collaborative space where independent stylists come together to create exceptional hair experiences. Founded on the principles of creativity, expertise, and genuine care for our clients, we've built a community of passionate professionals.

Located on Cowbridge Road East in the heart of Cardiff, our salon brings together stylists of varying specialties—from color specialists and cutting experts to styling professionals. Each stylist brings their own unique flair and clientele, creating a dynamic, welcoming environment.

We believe in putting you first. Book directly with your favorite stylist, enjoy personalized consultations, and experience hair care that truly understands your needs.
