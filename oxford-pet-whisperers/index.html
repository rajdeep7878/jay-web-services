Our Story

We Understand Your Dog

Oxford Pet Whisperers was founded on the belief that every dog deserves expert care, patient handling, and a safe space where they can thrive. Our team of certified dog behaviourists and groomers specializes in anxious dogs, puppies, and dogs with special needs.
