Our Story

We're Pet People, Through and Through

The Pet Palace was founded on a simple belief: every pet deserves grooming with skill, patience, and genuine love. Our team of certified groomers treats each visit like it matters -- because it does. We use only natural, hypoallergenic products and take the time to understand your pet's temperament, coat type, and individual needs. From anxious rescues to pampered pooches, we create a safe, welcoming environment where your pet can relax and enjoy their spa day.
